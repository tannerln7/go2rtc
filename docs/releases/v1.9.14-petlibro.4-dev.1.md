# Native Transport Protocol Corrections

This prerelease contains substantial corrections to the PLAF203 transport
protocol based on analysis of the OEM firmware and vendor SDK. Automated tests
and cross-platform builds have passed, but the revised implementation has not
yet completed physical-device qualification. Feedback from PLAF203 users,
particularly those experiencing stream stalls or resolution degradation, is
welcome.

## Changes

- Track validated transport receipt independently of H.264/AAC decoding, so
  unusable media does not create false ACK holes.
- Correctly identify native AV reset requests and correlate their responses.
  These operations reset transport sequence state; they do not select resolution.
- Preserve actual sequence and receipt tracking across bootstrap and runtime.
- Implement applicable sender-clear and receiver-reset semantics, independently
  of reliable-control acknowledgements.
- Correct normal/timed fragment decoding, 16-bit fragment counts, retransmission
  validation, and fragmented AAC assembly.
- Align transport ACK scheduling with the native 100 ms gate and prompt
  reliable-control progress reports.
- Remove obsolete startup sequence heuristics and capture-specific reset
  assumptions. No new runtime modes or feature flags are required.

These changes address underlying defects that could contribute to persistent
ACK gaps, retransmission problems, and streaming degradation. They do **not**
establish that the exact failure reported in PR #1 is resolved. Genuine missing
packets are not acknowledged or abandoned merely because they are old.

`quality=hd` remains an HD startup-readiness preference; `quality=sd` uses the
available stream without that wait. Neither forces the camera encoder's
resolution. See [the native transport contract](https://github.com/tannerln7/go2rtc/blob/v1.9.14-petlibro.4-dev.1/docs/petlibro-transport.md)
for protocol evidence and remaining limitations.

## Testing and feedback

Please include the release version, selected camera options, observed resolution
changes, and sanitized startup-to-failure logs. Bidirectional packet captures are
especially helpful for distinguishing real loss from parsing or ACK problems.
Do not post credentials, device identifiers, private addresses, or camera media;
follow the [debugging guide](https://github.com/tannerln7/go2rtc/blob/v1.9.14-petlibro.4-dev.1/docs/debugging.md) before sharing diagnostics.

The standalone Linux binaries and architecture-specific archives below are for
controlled testing. Archives include `go2rtc`, `petlibro-resolve`, and license
notices; `SHA256SUMS` covers the published assets. This development prerelease
does not replace the stable Petlibro release.
